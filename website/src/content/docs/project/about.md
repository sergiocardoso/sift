Sift is built and maintained by **Sérgio Cardoso**, a Senior Software Engineer based in São Paulo, Brazil, with over 20 years of experience building software in production.

## Background

Sérgio's career spans two decades: from webcasting and live-streaming infrastructure (FFmpeg, encoding, transcoding, VOD) at TVAOVIVO, to corporate dashboards and risk/credit analysis tools at Boa Nota, to editorial products, legal-data pipelines, and AI automation at [JOTA](https://www.jota.info), where he's worked since 2021 — moving from frontend into full-stack engineering, data pipelines, and applied AI.

His current focus sits at the intersection of software engineering, product, and applied AI: agent-assisted engineering and specification-driven development — turning business problems into architecture, context, acceptance criteria, and a working system, while retaining direct technical ownership of code quality, observability, and production operations.

## Stack

React, Next.js, TypeScript, Python, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Flutter, n8n, Docker, GCP, FFmpeg, Whisper, and AI/LLM tooling (agents, RAG, embeddings, vector databases, local models) — Sift's own choices (Rust, no mandatory network access, no mandatory AI) reflect the same engineering instinct: pragmatic, production-grade, and unafraid to say no to unnecessary complexity.

## Other work

Beyond client and employer work, Sérgio ships his own products end to end — Precivo (cost/pricing for makers, Flutter), Healthy Chew (mindful eating, Flutter), Caminho Solo (earnings tracking for gig drivers), Cancelo (free-trial reminders, React Native/Expo), and Alerta Edital (AI-driven government procurement monitoring), among others — listed with links on [sergiocardoso.dev](https://www.sergiocardoso.dev). Sift itself is an [open-source](https://github.com/sergiocardoso/sift) project out of **Flokin**, his lab for independent products and experimentation.
